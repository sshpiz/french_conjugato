Why practise verbs?

Knowing a word and finding its form mid-sentence are different things. VerbsFirst gives you a small, repeatable way to practise that recall: try an answer, reveal it, and check. The aim is less hesitation over familiar forms.

Verbs are also useful starting points for patterns: who does what, to whom, and with which preposition. Examples help connect the form to a meaning. Use this alongside listening, reading and real conversations.

Further reading

  • Roediger & Karpicke (2006): Test-Enhanced Learning — research into retrieval and later retention.
Privacy, speech & offline use

No account is required. Practice settings and progress are stored in your browser. Clearing site data can remove them; they do not automatically sync between devices.

Open the app online first. The apps can cache practice resources for later use, but not every resource is available offline. Audio depends on your device, installed voices and any optional downloads. Test your chosen app offline before relying on it.

Speech is optional. You can answer aloud without turning on the microphone. If you enable speech recognition, your browser or speech provider may process audio on its servers. Some voices require an internet connection or a download.

Some requests leave your device. This homepage uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. The language apps do not include analytics tracking. Hosting still receives normal web requests, and apps may load external fonts, scripts or voice files. Contact Support with any questions.
