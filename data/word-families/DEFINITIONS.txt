Word-family definitions and gender — dictionary-linked draft

English Wiktionary contributors: https://en.wiktionary.org/
French Wiktionnaire contributors: https://fr.wiktionary.org/
Dictionary-derived material is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Source URLs and revision IDs are retained in definitions.*.json and evidence.*.json.
View a snapshot with https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?oldid=REVISION_ID
(or fr.wiktionary.org for French Wiktionnaire evidence).

English definitions are selected from dictionary senses. Missing or long senses
are translated/summarized with local gemma3:12b and targeted editorial corrections.
Statuses distinguish dictionary, model-assisted and editorially corrected entries.
Gender comes from dictionary headwords where available; inferred values are marked.
The dataset is a draft and has not had exhaustive human linguistic review.

Spanish/Catalan: up to 12 highest-frequency dictionary-linked candidates per
source verb, excluding ordinary inflected forms, archaic/obsolete-only entries
and unsupported parts of speech. Source coverage is incomplete; empty records
mean no retained dictionary family, not that no family exists.

Approximate word-form frequencies: wordfreq 3.1.1, not sense-specific counts.
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq — accompanying attribution and license retained.

UI: words remain untranslated until tapped; nouns show m/f gender markers.
